% native    = both (default) | sindhi | urdu | none
% mono      = greyscale, for black-and-white printers
% foldmarks = DIN fold and punch marks
% sign      = 1 adds the signature image, 0 leaves the space for a pen
% closing   = left (default) | right  -- side for closing, signature and name
\documentclass[sign=1,closing=left]{saus-letter}

\usepackage{booktabs}

%% ---- Sender ---------------------------------------------------------------
\sausdepartment{Department of Computer Science}   % office name under the motto
\sausemail{hod.cs@saus.edu.pk}                    % shown in the footer
% \sausphone{...}                                 % a direct line replaces the main number
\setkomavar{fromname}{Head of Department}
\setkomavar{signature}{Head of Department\\ Department of Computer Science}
% Signature image. This demo uses a drawn specimen -- a real signature belongs
% in the signer's own copy of a letter, never in a shared template.
% The default is signature-trans.png next to this file; [height] is optional.
\saussignimage{assets/signature-specimen.png}

%% ---- This letter ----------------------------------------------------------
\setkomavar{myref}{SAUS/CS/2026/01}               % "Ref. No." -- same line as the date; {0} hides it
\setkomavar{subject}{University templates for lectures, posters and letters}

\begin{document}
\begin{letter}{%
  All Faculty Members\\
  Department of Computer Science\\
  The Shaikh Ayaz University, Shikarpur}

\opening{Dear Colleagues,}

The university's IT Directorate has prepared a set of document templates that
give our presentations, research posters and correspondence one consistent
identity. They are built on the university crest, use the same colours and
typefaces throughout, and set the university's name in English, Sindhi and
Urdu. I would like the department to adopt them for its teaching, research and
official work.

The templates are written in \LaTeX{}, which many of you already use for
research papers and theses:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Slides} --- a presentation theme for lectures and seminars, with
        mathematics set in a typeface matched to the text.
  \item \textbf{Posters} --- research posters from A3 to A0, in portrait or
        landscape, for conferences and the department's research events.
  \item \textbf{Letters} --- this letterhead, for the department's official
        correspondence and notifications.
\end{itemize}

All three share one brand file, so the university's colours, address and
typefaces stay the same across every document the department produces.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Template} & \textbf{Start from} & \textbf{Main file} \\
  \midrule
  Slides  & \texttt{saus-slides-starter.tex} & \texttt{beamerthemeSAUS.sty} \\
  Posters & \texttt{saus-poster-starter.tex} & \texttt{saus-poster.cls} \\
  Letters & \texttt{saus-letter-starter.tex} & \texttt{saus-letter.cls} \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The templates run on any standard \LaTeX{} installation, including Overleaf,
and need no fonts to be installed on individual computers. Colleagues who have
not used \LaTeX{} before can start from the starter files, which contain every
field they need to fill in.

Please use the slide template for lectures and seminars from the coming
semester, and the poster template for any research you present on behalf of the
department. Course coordinators are requested to share this letter with the
visiting faculty and teaching assistants in their courses.

The IT Directorate is glad to help anyone setting up the templates. Questions
about their use in the department may be sent to me at the address below.

\closing{Yours sincerely,}

\encl{Template guide (README)}
\cc{IT Directorate\\ Office file}

\end{letter}
\end{document}
